EduWand is a school platform that brings admissions and classroom teaching into one app — an Enrolment Growth Engine for front desk and counsellors, and an AI-assisted classroom for teachers and students.

EduWand is built by **Fovea Infotech** for **LessonForge**.

## What EduWand does

- **Admissions** — track enquiries through a pipeline, schedule follow-ups, and message parents, without juggling spreadsheets.
- **Lesson Studio** — teachers plan lessons, generate materials, and build assignments with AI assistance, always reviewed before it reaches a class.
- **Grading & attainment** — assignments are graded with AI support and released to students once a teacher approves them, with class-level insight into where a topic needs a recap.
- **AI assistant** — a built-in assistant answers questions about your own data and can prepare changes (like scheduling a follow-up) for you to confirm.
