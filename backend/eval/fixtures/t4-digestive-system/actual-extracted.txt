[Figure] A simplified anatomical illustration of the human digestive system, depicting the path food takes from the mouth to the anus. Key organs visible include the oral cavity, pharynx, esophagus, liver (red organ), stomach (light orange), gallbladder (small green under liver), pancreas (yellow behind stomach), small intestine (coiled orange in center), large intestine (green tube surrounding small intestine), and rectum/anus (blue gradient tube at the bottom). The diagram shows the relative positions and connections of these organs as a continuous tract.
